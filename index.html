Why ranges, not fixed prices

Size sets the canvas, but detail, color saturation, and placement set the hours — and hours set the price. A quote is locked in at consultation.

Deposits & rebooking

Deposits are non-refundable but transfer once if you reschedule with 48+ hours' notice. No-shows forfeit the deposit and require a new one to rebook.
